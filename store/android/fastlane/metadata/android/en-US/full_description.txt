OpenTable brings 13 timeless tabletop and strategy games to Android in a fast, deterministic package powered by Rust and Godot/Redot.

Included Games:
• Chess (Full FIDE rules, PGN/FEN export, Opening Practice)
• Go (19×19 board, Ko rule, SGF export)
• Shogi (9×9 board, piece drops, promotions)
• Xiangqi (9×10 board, Palace, River & Flying General)
• Checkers (8×8 mandatory captures & multi-jump chains)
• Chinese Checkers (121-cell star board)
• Backgammon (24 points, Bar, Bearing Off & Doubling Cube)
• Battleship (Fleet placement & tactical salvo grid)
• Ludo (4-player race with safe stars & captures)
• Mille Bornes (Road-race card game with Coup-Fourré)
• Monopoly (Properties, houses/hotels, auctions & mortgages)
• Uno (Action cards, Wild +4 & colorblind geometric icons)
• Crisis / TEG (30 territories, 6 continents & secret objectives)

Key Features:
• Play Offline vs Local Minimax AI (Easy, Medium, Hard) or Pass-and-Play on one device.
• Nearby Connections P2P & Online WebSocket Relay rooms (6-character room codes).
• Touch Pinch-to-Zoom & Panning on dense boards, plus Colorblind Accessibility Mode.
• Full Match Replays, Undo/Redo, and Local Save Slots.
